❓ Frequently Asked Questions

How should I choose which lottery to play?
Compare jackpot size, draw frequency, and odds of winning. Powerball and Mega Millions offer huge jackpots but low odds, while Taiwan's Daily Cash (539) has better odds and suits smaller, casual bets.
What's the difference between Asian lotteries and Powerball or Mega Millions?
They use different number ranges and rules. Powerball and Mega Millions are US games with typically larger jackpots — legal cross-border participation requires an authorized channel.
How often is SoftGlow's draw data updated?
Results are pulled automatically from each lottery's official source every day, updated as soon as possible after each draw, alongside historical records and number statistics.
Can number generators actually improve my odds of winning?
Number generators are mainly for quickly creating a combination, avoiding duplicate picks, or reviewing historical frequency — draws are random, so no method can guarantee a win.
Can I buy lottery tickets directly on this site?
SoftGlow provides draw results, historical statistics, and free number generators — we don't sell tickets. Please place bets through your local authorized lottery retailer.
